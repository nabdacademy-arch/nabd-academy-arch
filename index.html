About NABD

Education built around access, service and scientific curiosity.

NABD Academy for Medical Sciences provides online educational activities focused primarily on theoretical and non-clinical medical sciences. The initiative seeks to support students through accessible learning, volunteer-led education and an expanding academic community.

Our Purpose

To make high-quality educational opportunities in medical sciences more accessible while encouraging curiosity, collaboration, responsibility and continuous learning among students.

Quality & Transparency

Clear about what we provide.

Transparency is central to responsible educational development. NABD distinguishes educational participation from formal academic degrees, professional licensing and external accreditation.

Educational Scope

Activities are primarily theoretical, educational and non-clinical.

Completion Records
